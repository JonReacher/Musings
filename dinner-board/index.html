Items, prices & minimumsEvery ingredient, including ones not on this week's list. Edits apply everywhere.

Lunches. Each Sunday–Thursday dinner has a “Pack work lunch” checkbox that adds one serving for the next workday. It starts ticked for dinners marked “Lunch” (untick “Pack work lunch” on any day to skip it). A leftover night before a workday adds its lunches to the batch cooked the night before. The grocery list shows these as “+lunch”.

Minimums. Each item can have a minimum buy (the smallest package outside Costco, like a 1 lb pack of ground beef or an 8-count of naan), and Costco items buy whole packs. When that leaves some unused, the item gets a warning triangle with how much is left over and roughly what it costs. Set minimums on the list, in a dinner’s Ingredients panel, or under Items, prices & minimums.

How the store is picked. An item goes on the Costco list only when this week's dinners use most of a Costco pack, so nothing big has to be stored: at least 75% of the pack for fresh meat, produce and frozen food, 50% for fridge items that keep for weeks (eggs, cheese, butter, bacon), and 15% for shelf-stable pantry items. Everything else goes to the store you usually buy it from (H‑E‑B unless set otherwise; meat is never assigned to Walmart). Change an item's store from its menu on the list or in a dinner's Ingredients panel.

Prices are rough estimates, not live prices. Costco chicken, eggs and rotisserie chicken were checked against Nov 2026 sources; the rest are my best guesses. Type over any price, here or in a dinner's Ingredients panel, and it updates everywhere that item is used. Costco prices are entered per pack. Per‑dinner costs count only what each meal uses; the trip total is what you'll actually pay, with whole packs, cans and bunches.
