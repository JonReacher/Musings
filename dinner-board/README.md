# Family Dinner Board

A single-page weekly dinner planner and grocery list. Open `index.html` in any browser. There is nothing to install.

## What it does

- **Plan the week.** Pick a dinner for each night from 40 built-in dinners and 17 sides, by tapping "Choose dinner" or by dragging on a computer. Mark leftover nights, easy nights and packed work lunches.
- **Fill the week for you.** Weather (Hot, Warm, Cold), Cheap week, Make easy, Leftovers and Redo suggest dinners. Each one needs a second tap before it changes anything.
- **Lock the week.** Freeze the plan so nothing can change it by accident. Recipes and the grocery checkboxes still work.
- **Recipes.** Every dinner and side has a recipe with a short description, ingredients and steps, sized to the number of people you're feeding.
- **Grocery run.** The shopping list is built from the recipes, scaled to your head count and leftover nights, and split across Costco, H-E-B, Aldi and Walmart. Tick what you already have and it drops out of the total.

## Notes

- Your week, prices and checked items are saved in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere, and each person who opens the page gets their own copy.
- Prices are rough estimates. Edit them in the grocery list or in the full price list at the bottom of the page.
- Recipes adapted from Allrecipes, Gimme Some Oven, Natasha's Kitchen, Skinnytaste, Budget Bytes, iFoodReal, Recipe Diaries, Le Crème de la Crumb, The Girl on Bloor and The Frugal Navy Wife are credited inside the app with links to the originals.
